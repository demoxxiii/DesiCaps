FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch. DesiCaps runs on your computer, so there are no server costs to pass on to you. It's open source, with no watermark, no limits and no account.

Does it need the internet?

No. Transcription, editing and rendering all work offline. Internet is only used if you choose to download an extra language (Hindi Devanagari / English) or the optional local AI.

How accurate is the Hinglish?

Very good on clear speech, but no AI is perfect. Type names and brand words in the “Names in video” box (e.g. “GTA 6, Rockstar”) and DesiCaps spells them right; anything else takes a double-click to fix.

Will it run on my laptop?

Yes, on most Windows 10/11 PCs from the last ~10 years and any Apple Silicon Mac. A graphics card makes transcription much faster; without one it still works on the CPU, just slower.

Do I need After Effects?

No. DesiCaps renders the finished MP4 itself. The After Effects export is there for editors who want to fine-tune animations in AE.

What are “AI Style” and “Post kit”?

Optional extras powered by a free local AI (Ollama). AI Style colours the punch words and adds emoji; Post kit writes hook titles, captions and hashtags in your Hinglish tone. The app guides you through the one-time setup.

Is there a phone app?

Yes — DesiCaps for Android (Android 10+) makes the captions on the phone itself, with the Hinglish model built in and bigger models as optional downloads. Longer videos are faster on a PC. An iPhone version may come later.

Intel Mac?

Not in this version — the Mac app needs an Apple Silicon (M-series) Mac.
